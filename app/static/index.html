    Glossary

    AUC24
    Total vancomycin exposure over 24 hours at steady state (mg·h/L). The 2020 consensus guideline targets 400–600 when the MIC is 1 mg/L.
    Above 800 mg·h/L
    Exposure associated with a higher risk of acute kidney injury.
    Peak and trough
    Highest concentration (end of infusion) and lowest concentration (just before the next dose), in mg/L.
    Clearance
    How fast the patient removes vancomycin (L/h). AUC24 = daily dose ÷ clearance, so clearance drives every dosing decision.
    Covariate-only estimate
    A prediction from age, weight, kidney function and ICU status alone, used before any levels are available.
    Bayesian estimate
    The covariate estimate updated with the patient's own measured levels through a two-compartment pharmacokinetic model.
    Chance within target
    Probability that the true AUC24 falls inside the target range, given the uncertainty of the estimate.
    Course
    A period on one regimen. A new course starts whenever the dose, interval or infusion time changes.
    Simulated levels
    For these synthetic patients only: follow-up levels generated from the simulator so the journey can be explored. Real levels replace them in practice.
